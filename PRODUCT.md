# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People who need a quick, private image or PDF format conversion — designers, marketers, and anyone sharing files — usually on a laptop or phone, often mid-task. They want results without uploading to a third-party site.

## Product Purpose

convetin.dg converts images and PDFs locally in the browser (PNG, JPEG, WebP, PDF). Success is: pick files → choose output → download, with optional compression, and zero data leaving the device.

## Brand Personality

Technical, precise, quiet confidence. Pixel-terminal craft from the akhsanhakiki Light Pixel system — not playful SaaS, not corporate enterprise.

## Anti-references

Generic HeroUI / gradient SaaS dashboards, purple-on-white AI templates, rounded card stacks with icon badges, cream-terracotta editorial defaults, dark neon “dev tool” clichés.

## Design Principles

1. **Task first** — the converter is the page; brand chrome stays secondary.
2. **Local trust** — reinforce privacy through calm UI, not loud marketing.
3. **Progressive complexity** — compression and advanced options stay collapsed until needed.
4. **One composition** — avoid nested panels and competing visual systems.
5. **Touch-ready** — primary actions and controls work cleanly on narrow screens.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA target. Visible focus rings, reduced-motion support, contrast-safe muted text, keyboard-operable upload and controls.
